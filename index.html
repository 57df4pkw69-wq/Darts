Press and hold anywhere on the board area. The aiming ring floats above your thumb and drifts like a real hand, and it gets shakier the longer you hold. Let go to throw. 501 and 301 must finish on a double or the bull.

Straight knockout. Every match is best of 3 sets, and each set is best of 5 legs, with the throw alternating each leg. The rest of the field are the pros, each playing to their real average. Matches between two pros are played out off-stage. The game is 501, or 301 if you picked it on the main menu.
